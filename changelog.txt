Update 1.0.4.6 - Anniversary Update Hotfix 2

Additions:
None

Reworks:
Added damage scaling with charge to the Stun Gun (up to x5 damage) and made it so fully charged hits inflict Electrified for 5 seconds and Galvanic Corrosion for 1 second
Starfish of the Depths now always creates an ink bolt even when only hitting once and not bouncing, and increased shot speed stat (3-25)
Starfish of the Depths now scales with shoot speed instead of being fixed, essentially increasing shoot speed (17.5-25)

Removals:
None

Balance:
Increased Stun Gun damage (20-25) and attack speed (71-60)
Increased Seared Shredder projectile range (200-250)
Made Hell's Bells inflict Hellfire instead of On Fire and increased their movement speed (0.05-0.15)
Increased Starfish of the Depths damage (150-200) but also increased damage loss per bounce (0.8-0.75)

Changes:
Slightly improved the tooltips and functionality of the Shattered Subcommunity.

Fixes:
None

Localization Changes:
None
