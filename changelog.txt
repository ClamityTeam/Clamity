Update 1.0.4.4 - Annyversary Update

Now Cataclysm Mod 3 year old. Time to release this small gift.

Additions:
Added Evercold Cyan rarity for all post-Wall of Bronze items. Analogue of Calamity Red for Calamitas and Exotic Rainbow for Exo Mechs but for Wall of Bronze content.
Added Shattered Subcommunity. Like Shattered Community he uses full power of The Subcommunity + leveling system. He level ups overtime by having active "Calm Mode" and every level increase passive calm generation.

Reworks:
Reworked Greatest Peak (previosly Frozen Volcano). Now he in-line with Hellkite. Charged attack creates lingering AoE damage.
Reworked Subzero Slicer (previosly Frozen Star Shuriken). Now he homes to target and creates frost storm on it. Stealth strike creates 3 slicer and has bigger mist.
Redesigned Endobsidian Bar (previosly Enchanted Metal)
Reworked Seared Shredder. Has custom swing and throw short range spinning flaming scythe that returns back after a short time

Removals:
Removed Everest for future rework. (rework not completed yet)
